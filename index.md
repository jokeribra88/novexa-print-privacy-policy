# Novexa Print Research — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

## 1. About this application

Novexa Print Research is an internal research application operated by Novexa Print. It is intended for use by its owner to support market and trend research for digital and print-on-demand products.

It is not a public service and is not intended for use by external customers.

## 2. Information and services

The application may connect to Pinterest through its official API, but only for functions and data access explicitly authorized by Pinterest.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information from other Pinterest users or access private Pins or Boards.

## 3. API credentials and data handling

Pinterest API credentials are kept private and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Pinterest API data will be accessed, processed, stored, and retained only to the extent permitted by Pinterest's applicable terms, policies, and granted permissions.

Pinterest API data is not sold or distributed to third parties.

## 4. Local processing

Research processing is intended to take place on the owner's local computer. Any handling of third-party data will remain subject to the applicable provider's terms and restrictions.

## 5. Third-party services

The application may interact with third-party services, including Pinterest.

Those services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices. Please consult their respective policies for further information.

The privacy policy webpage may be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation, subject to GitHub's policies.

## 6. Data security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect application credentials and research data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.
