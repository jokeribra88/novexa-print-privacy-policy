Novexa Print Local Delivery

Private internal business application

Novexa Print Local Delivery supports internal research workflows for Novexa Print, an Etsy-focused digital-product and print-on-demand business. It is not a consumer application and is not offered for public sign-up.

What the application does

The application helps the owner organize keyword research, marketplace opportunity findings, and research report delivery for review on a local computer.

  • Collect and compare permitted keyword-planning and research information from authorized data sources.
  • Maintain a source-aware record of estimates, observations, and unknown fields without treating estimates as proven sales.
  • Use explicitly authorized Google Drive files for controlled research-report transfer to a private local environment.
  • Support human review of opportunities before any product is created or listed.

Use of Google services

Google APIs are used only for the features and permissions granted by the account owner. Google Drive access is limited to the files authorized for the application. Google Ads API keyword-planning access, if approved, will be used for internal analysis of Google search-demand data; these metrics are not presented as Etsy search volumes or verified sales.

Availability and access

This is an owner-operated internal application. Access to local research systems and authenticated Google data is not offered through this informational website.
